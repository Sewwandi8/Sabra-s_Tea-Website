About Us

Our Story..

Sabra's Tea began in 2020, sparked by love for tea and a commitment to quality.

We wanted to create tea that people could truly enjoy, sip by sip. Every leaf is carefully chosen from the most fertile regions in Sabaragamuwa province, Sri Lanka, bringing you a taste that’s both pure and full of flavor.

From the classic black and green teas to unique herbal blends, each cup offers a moment of relaxation and a taste of authenticity.

Why Choose Sabra's Tea?

Our dedication to quality and authenticity makes us stand out.

Each blend is designed to suit different tastes and occasions.

With Sabra's Tea, every sip becomes an experience, every cup a moment to savor.
